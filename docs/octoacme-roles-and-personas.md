# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own test strategy, quality assurance, and acceptance validation. They ensure that delivered features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing approach and test plans for features
- Create and maintain automated test suites (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Execute manual QA and acceptance testing when needed
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before marking work as done
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Collaborate with developers on test coverage and bug severity

### Goals
- Ensure every release meets quality and acceptance standards
- Reduce production defects and customer-impacting issues
- Maintain high test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement (acceptance criteria clarity)
- Sprint planning (QA effort estimation)
- Daily standups (test blocker status)
- PR reviews (test coverage feedback)
- Release readiness sign-off

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, bug triage, and acceptance criteria clarity
- **Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance and quality expectations
- **Project Managers**: Report on quality metrics and release readiness status

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve initiatives, and remove organizational blockers. They represent customer needs and business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Articulate business problems and desired outcomes
- Approve project charter and budget
- Provide visibility into business priorities
- Remove organizational blockers and dependencies
- Participate in key gates (initiation, release)
- Receive and act on status and risk escalations

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment across business and technical teams
- Remove external constraints to delivery

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Escalation meetings for blockers
- Release announcements and feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define business priorities and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Receive escalations and approve critical decisions
- **Developers**: Provide context on business objectives and constraints

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define technical direction, review design decisions, and ensure solutions align with long-term technical strategy. They mentor developers and mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Conduct technical design reviews and provide feedback
- Identify and mitigate technical debt and risks
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Evaluate technology trade-offs and recommendations
- Ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk and complexity
- Foster a culture of engineering excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and RFC (Request for Comments) discussions
- Architecture decision documentation
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Risk and technical debt assessments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, design feedback, and mentorship
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and dependencies
- **Product Managers**: Communicate technical constraints and feasibility

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, monitoring, and incident response. They ensure systems are reliable, performant, and secure in production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and infrastructure
- Configure monitoring, alerting, and observability tools
- Manage production environments and handle incident response
- Implement automation and reduce manual toil
- Ensure backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity
- Collaborate on performance optimization and scalability

### Goals
- Maintain high system availability and reliability
- Enable fast, safe deployments to production
- Reduce operational burden through automation

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination and deployment planning
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and performance metrics
- On-call escalations and alerts

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinate deployments, infrastructure needs, and observability requirements
- **Project Managers**: Report on deployment readiness and production health
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Support staging environment setup and smoke test execution

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security standards, protect sensitive data, and comply with regulatory requirements. They assess risks and implement controls.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Ensure compliance with security policies and regulations
- Review code and architecture for security vulnerabilities
- Manage access controls and credential management
- Participate in incident response and security investigations
- Provide security training and guidance to teams

### Goals
- Protect customer data and system integrity
- Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
- Build a security-first culture

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during design and code phases
- Vulnerability assessments and risk reports
- Compliance audits and reporting
- Security incident response and notifications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide security guidance, code review feedback, and secure coding practices
- **Operations/DevOps Engineers**: Ensure secure infrastructure, access controls, and monitoring
- **Project Managers**: Assess security risks and timelines for remediation

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When planning projects, refer to these roles to ensure all necessary perspectives are represented in decision-making.
- Use the interaction descriptions to understand cross-functional collaboration points and communication needs.
